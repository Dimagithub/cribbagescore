Quick reference. Game to 121 (short game 61). Fifteen = 2. Pair = 2, three of a kind = 6, four of a kind = 12. Run = 1 per card. Flush = 4 (5 with the starter; crib needs 5). Nobs = 1. His heels = 2 to the dealer. Pegging: 15 = 2, 31 = 2, go = 1. Skunk: loser under 91. Double skunk: loser under 61. Mark the dealer each round in the Deal column and add your points to the running total.

How to use the sheet

  1. Write the player names at the top.
  2. Each round, mark who dealt in the Deal column (for example D1 or D2).
  3. Write the points each player scored during the play (Peg), for their hand (Hand), and for the dealer's crib (Crib).
  4. Add them to the running total. The first player to reach 121 wins, even in the middle of counting.
  5. If the loser finishes under 91 it is a skunk; under 61 is a double skunk.
